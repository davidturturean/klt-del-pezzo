# Citation and attribution

The mathematical manuscript is *Singular points of klt del Pezzo surfaces of Picard number one in positive characteristic*. The bundled artifact is the frozen 40-page version identified by the hashes in [source/README.md](source/README.md). Its author line is absent from the supplied source. This package does not infer manuscript authorship from repository ownership or commit metadata.

For work using the formalization, cite the repository title, the source commit `8b6110c192269d3c3b2d80c2e54bef9f25864865`, and checkpoint **candidate1559** dated 13 September 2026. A citation should identify this as an ongoing formalization and retain the four literature assumptions. An accepted supporting lemma should be cited by its exact declaration name and hypotheses.

The manuscript attributes the characteristic-three sharp example to Fabio Bernasconi and the unbounded characteristic-two family to Seán Keel and James McKernan. The retained attribution review verifies the numerical correspondence for the characteristic-two family; the two constructions start from different ambient surfaces, and an isomorphism between their contracted surfaces has not been established by that review. Its related-work section also records the announced characteristic-specific bounds of Palka and Pełka. The repository does not claim priority over those constructions or announcements. The bibliography in the paper gives the complete references.

The four admitted Stacks statements and their source pin are listed in [TRUST.md](notes/TRUST.md). Compatibility modules preserve third-party author and source notices. Attribution for code reuse is separate from attribution for a mathematical result.

No project-wide license is assigned in this staging package. Existing third-party notices remain in force for the material they cover. Authorship metadata and licenses for original code, documentation and the manuscript must be settled before a public release is finalized.
