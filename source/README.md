# Manuscript

[Read the 40-page PDF](manuscript.pdf) or inspect the [LaTeX source](manuscript.tex).

The frozen manuscript is titled *Singular points of klt del Pezzo surfaces of Picard number one in positive characteristic*. It is by David Turturean and is formalized in this repository: every numbered statement has a Lean counterpart, mapped in [PAPER_TO_LEAN.md](../notes/PAPER_TO_LEAN.md).

The paper and source are preserved exactly as supplied to the formalization project. The source was first tracked in commit `c0062ccf35b75c49989ad94efa66a519b4665b91` on 6 September 2026. Its bibliography and prior-work discussion are retained.
