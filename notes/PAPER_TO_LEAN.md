### lem:tree-picard

Picard group of a rational tree

**Accepted contract.** Let Z be a connected reduced nodal curve whose irreducible components are copies of P^1 and whose dual graph is a tree; the multidegree map Pic(Z) -> Z^{components} is an isomorphism, and in particular a line bundle of degree zero on every component is trivial. Lean: for an algebraically closed field k and a reduced Noetherian scheme X locally of finite type over k with topologicalKrullDim X <= 1, tree-shaped component-point incidence graph, transverse component branches at every node (the intrinsic nodality predicate), and identifications e C : Z_C ≅ P^1 of every component, multidegreeHom k X e is bijective (rationalTreePicard), giving X.Pic ≃* (components -> Multiplicative Z) (rationalTreePicardEquiv) and the degree-zero triviality clause (rationalTreePicard_trivial_of_degree_zero). The degree on a component is the transition exponent through e C (componentExponent).

**Scope boundaries.** Nodality is the intrinsic transverse-germ predicate HasTransverseComponentBranches (two branches with independent cotangent classes at every intersection point); its derivation from the completed-stalk form k[[x,y]]/(xy) or from an SNC embedding is a separate interface obligation. The degree on a component is the accepted transition exponent componentExponent through the chosen identification e C; its comparison with the Cartier/line-bundle degree of lane D's F03 API is a separate obligation. The plan correction of docs/GAPS.md applies: reducible curves allowed, no ambient surface assumed. The exponent is the integer of the accepted projectiveLinePicardExponentEquiv : Pic(P¹) ≃* ℤ normalised by monomialLineBundle_exponent; both conclusions are invariant under its sign.

**Recorded open clauses.** Interface obligations above (nodality from a completed-stalk/SNC form; identification of the transition exponent with a divisor degree, including the sign convention); the forest (disjoint-union) variant of THEOREM_MAP strategy item 3 is not stated — X must be connected (IsTree); no accepted nonvacuity witness instantiates the hypotheses yet; §2 consumers not stated.

### F28

Frobenius graph as a k-morphism and arbitrary selected points

**Accepted contract.** For every algebraically closed field k of characteristic p > 0: the k-linear coordinate-power morphism [x0:x1] -> [x0^p:x1^p] on the actual P^1 is a morphism over Spec k acting as a -> a^p on rational points, and its absolute Frobenius factorisation is recorded; its graph is a closed subscheme of P^1 x_k P^1 isomorphic to P^1 as a section over the first factor; the class of O(graph) in the actual Picard group of P^1 x P^1 is p a + b for the actual fibre classes; for every n there are n distinct closed k-rational points on the graph, and any injective family of sections gives distinct closed points with residue field k; each rational point has contact of order p with its b-fibre.
